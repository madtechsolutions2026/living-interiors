## Daylight generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: main hero photograph for The Living Interiors interior design website. Create one exceptionally beautiful, photorealistic editorial architectural photograph of a warm contemporary Indian urban living room, earthy organic luxury inspired by caramel, clay, cream, and dark espresso. This must be a complete room, NOT close-up faces or wall art. Portrait composition 4:5 for right half of split website hero; all key objects should remain legible in square center crop on mobile. Eye-level camera with corrected architectural verticals, real spatial depth, inviting professionally styled home rather than showroom. Composition: sculptural cream curved sofa dominates lower middle; organically rounded travertine coffee table with a small handmade terracotta vase and a book in foreground; warm oak fluted cabinet and a tall softly rounded architectural opening in rear center; linen curtains and daylight from right; one leafy indoor olive tree on right edge; a sculptural aged brass pendant visible in upper middle. Restrained ivory plaster wall, natural woven rug, cushions in clay and cocoa. Luxurious tactile finishes, realistic stitching, subtle wear and material variation. Soft late afternoon sunlight and gentle shadow, airy natural grading with warm neutrals, no yellow/orange wash. High-end magazine cover quality, crisp focus, very believable lighting. No humans, no faces or sculptural heads, no text, no watermark, no collage, no website UI. This is a new imaginary concept interior, not an actual client project.

## Evening edit prompt

Use case: lighting-weather edit. Asset type: evening state of an interactive interior website hero. Edit target: the referenced living room image. Change ONLY time of day and lighting to a beautifully atmospheric blue-hour evening. Preserve camera, image dimensions and framing, every object and its exact location, all furniture, table, sofa, vase, leaves, brass pendant, rug, wood cabinet, arch and curtains unchanged. Window outside becomes subtle twilight blue, the brass pendant is switched on with a warm believable light casting a soft pool over sofa and table. Gentle ambient warm interior light, sophisticated and inviting, clear visible materials and forms, not dark or muddy, no heavy orange wash. Match the geometry and crop exactly so it can crossfade with the daylight version without objects moving. No new objects, no text, no watermark.
